### 👋 Welcome to Rakolto

Collecting financial documents is universally the most painful part of getting a loan closed. Borrowers get overwhelmed by endless email threads, and brokers waste hours manually chasing missing files.

**Rakolto fixes that.** We give loan officers a single, intelligent portal that turns document chasing into a seamless, stress-free experience for everyone.
